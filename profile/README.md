# Computational Kinetics Group

Welcome to the GitHub organization of the **Computational Kinetics Group (CKG)** at **ETH Zürich**.

We develop theoretical models and computational methods for fluid dynamics with a particular focus on approaches rooted in **kinetic theory**, including the **lattice Boltzmann method (LBM)** and discrete-velocity kinetic methods.

**[Website](https://ckg.ethz.ch/) · [Research](https://ckg.ethz.ch/research.html) · [Publications](https://ckg.ethz.ch/publications.html) · [Group Members](https://ckg.ethz.ch/the-group/group-members.html) · [ETH Zürich](https://ethz.ch/)**

Our research spans fundamental kinetic theory, numerical-method development, and applications to complex flow problems.

## Research

Current research directions include:

* **Kinetic theory of gases** and the connection between kinetic and macroscopic descriptions of fluids
* **Lattice Boltzmann methods** and discrete kinetic modelling
* **High-speed and compressible flows**
* **Non-ideal and multiphase flows**
* **Multicomponent and reactive flows**
* **Flows in porous media**
* Development of stable, accurate, and computationally efficient kinetic solvers

## Open Research Software

This organization hosts open-source software, data, and supporting material developed within CKG.

Repositories may include:

* source code accompanying scientific publications;
* datasets and scripts used to reproduce published results;
* reference implementations of kinetic and lattice Boltzmann models;
* numerical solvers and computational frameworks;
* benchmark and validation cases;
* examples, utilities, and research prototypes.

For publication-related repositories, please refer to the individual repository for the associated paper, documentation, recommended citation, and instructions for reproducing the reported results.

## Reproducibility and Citation

We aim to make the computational methods underlying our research transparent and reproducible wherever possible.

If you use software or data from this organization in academic work, please cite the corresponding publication and, where provided, the software release or repository citation specified in its `CITATION.cff` file.
